## Purpose and scope

Meridian Group uses role-based work arrangements to balance customer service, collaboration, accessibility, security and individual flexibility. This policy applies to employees and long-term contingent workers unless a contract, collective agreement or local law provides a more protective rule. It does not guarantee that every role can be performed from every location.

Roles are classified as site-dependent, hybrid or remote-eligible. Classification reflects the work, not seniority or manager preference. Site-dependent work includes duties requiring physical equipment, secure facilities or in-person service. Hybrid work combines approved remote work with planned on-site collaboration. Remote eligibility means the role may be performed away from a Meridian site, subject to employment, tax, security and customer constraints.

## Requirements

Teams publish normal working patterns and hybrid anchor days at least thirty days in advance. Managers may request additional on-site attendance for a documented customer event, incident response, training need or planning session, using reasonable notice. A recurring material change to a team's pattern requires HR Operations review and consistent treatment of comparable roles.

Employees keep their work location, emergency contact and working hours current. Remote locations must provide reasonable privacy, safe working conditions and reliable connectivity. Confidential conversations must not be conducted where unauthorized people can overhear them. Meridian-managed devices and approved remote-access controls remain mandatory under IT-001.

Attendance information may be used for workplace capacity, emergency response, travel planning and evaluating whether the operating model works. Badge events, collaboration telemetry or online presence must not be treated as a direct measure of individual productivity or used as the sole basis for performance action.

## Temporary arrangements and exceptions

A temporary arrangement records the reason category, start date, end or review date, expected hours, time-zone implications and security constraints. Line managers may approve routine arrangements up to thirty calendar days when there is no tax, immigration, customer or accommodation concern. Longer or cross-border arrangements require HR review before they begin.

Medical, disability, pregnancy or caregiving requests that may require accommodation follow HR-002. Managers receive only the information necessary to implement an approved restriction or adjustment. They must not request diagnostic details or promise an outcome before HR review.

## Responsibilities and evidence

Employees follow the agreed pattern and raise conflicts early. Managers plan work around outcomes, document material changes and avoid inconsistent exceptions. HR Operations owns classifications and exception records. IT and Information Security assess device, access and location risk. Facilities uses aggregate attendance data for site planning.

Evidence includes the current role classification, team pattern, approved temporary arrangement, review date and any linked accommodation decision. Informal chat does not permanently change a work arrangement. Failure to attend without notice is handled through normal attendance procedures, with wellbeing and accommodation factors considered before disciplinary conclusions.
