## Purpose and governing principle

Meridian Group provides a consistent framework for planned leave, unplanned absence, workplace accommodation and return-to-work support. Local statutory rights, contracts and collective arrangements prevail where they are more protective. Employees will not be penalized for requesting a lawful accommodation, reporting illness, using protected leave or raising a wellbeing concern in good faith.

## Leave notification and approval

Planned leave is requested through the HR system with enough notice for reasonable coverage. Managers decide ordinary requests using business coverage, prior commitments and fair treatment across the team; they must not rank requests by personal preference. Approval is recorded in the HR system. A calendar entry or chat acknowledgement alone is not the authoritative leave record.

For unplanned absence, the employee notifies the manager or designated contact as soon as reasonably possible and provides an expected update time when known. A family member or representative may notify Meridian when the employee cannot. Employees are not required to provide detailed medical information to a line manager. HR may request limited supporting documentation where lawful and necessary.

A release milestone, customer meeting or understaffed project does not by itself cancel protected or already approved leave. The team first uses handover, delegation, schedule adjustment and documented coverage. Any proposed cancellation or recall follows HR Operations review and applicable local rules.

## Accommodation process

An employee may request an adjustment verbally or in writing. Managers route the request promptly to HR and avoid deciding medical necessity themselves. HR conducts an interactive review focused on functional limitations, essential duties, effectiveness, duration and operational impact. Occupational Health, Legal, IT, Facilities or Security may advise only to the extent relevant.

Managers receive the approved adjustment and implementation instructions, not the underlying diagnosis. Accommodation records are restricted HR information. Approved measures may include schedule changes, equipment, communication formats, workplace changes, temporary duty modification or remote work. Each decision records an owner and review date; temporary approval does not silently become permanent or expire without review.

## Return to work and wellbeing

Return-to-work planning considers current capacity, safety, workload and confidentiality. A phased return must describe hours, duties, checkpoints and escalation contacts. Managers must not require an employee to perform work during certified leave. Voluntary social contact must not become pressure to monitor work.

Immediate safety concerns use emergency services or the published crisis route. General wellbeing resources are supportive services, not substitutes for emergency or clinical care. Retaliation, gossip about medical information and attempts to bypass approved restrictions are investigated under the conduct process.

## Records and accountability

The HR system retains request dates, approval status, leave category, necessary documentation, restrictions, implementation actions and reviews according to retention rules. Project systems should hold coverage actions, not medical details. HR Operations owns policy interpretation; Legal determines statutory conflicts; managers own practical coverage and respectful implementation.
