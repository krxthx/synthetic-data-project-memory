## Classification

Meridian information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Public information is approved for external release. Internal information is intended for Meridian and authorized partners. Confidential information could cause meaningful harm if disclosed, including non-public commercial, employee or customer material. Restricted information requires the strongest controls because disclosure or alteration could create severe legal, safety, financial or security impact.

The Data Owner determines classification using content, contract, law and business impact. A system label assists handling but does not make obviously sensitive information less sensitive. Combined datasets may require a higher classification than each input. Users challenge unclear labels before sharing rather than selecting the lowest convenient category.

## Handling and sharing

Access is limited to authorized recipients with a business need. Confidential and Restricted information uses approved storage, encryption and transfer mechanisms. External sharing identifies the recipient, purpose, duration and onward-use restrictions. Public links are not used for non-public content. Screens, recordings, printouts, exports and backups follow the same classification as the information they expose.

Suspected misdelivery, excessive access or public exposure is reported immediately. Recipients do not attempt broad cleanup without preserving evidence and coordinating with Security or Privacy.

## Retention and disposal

Records are retained according to record category, legal hold, contract and regulatory schedule—not inbox space or employee preference. The authoritative copy and owner are identified where duplicates exist. Convenience copies are deleted when no longer needed. A legal hold suspends ordinary deletion for covered information until Legal releases it.

Approved disposal makes information unreadable and includes relevant exports, removable media and managed backups according to the service design. Departing employees do not bulk-copy business records for personal retention.

## Generative AI

Only approved generative-AI services and approved use cases may process Meridian information. Restricted data is prohibited unless the use case has explicit Data Owner, Privacy, Legal and Information Security approval with technical controls. Removing names is not automatically sufficient; re-identification, sensitive attributes, contractual limits and provider terms still apply.

Prompts, retrieved context and uploaded files are treated as disclosures to the service. Users provide the minimum necessary information and respect intellectual-property and confidentiality duties. AI output is a draft or analytical aid. An accountable person verifies accuracy, provenance, permissions, bias, confidentiality and fitness before business use. AI may not make final employment, legal, safety, access or financial decisions without the approved human-controlled process.

Material AI-assisted content should retain the use case, model or service, date, reviewer and source references where needed for audit or reproducibility. Fabricated citations, hidden automated decisions and attempts to bypass tool restrictions are policy violations.

## Exceptions and accountability

The Data Owner, Privacy, Legal and Information Security jointly review exceptions involving Confidential or Restricted data. Approvals are scoped, time-bound and tied to stated controls. A project deadline, customer request or technically available integration is not by itself an exception. Violations may require access restriction, incident response, notification, retraining or disciplinary action.
