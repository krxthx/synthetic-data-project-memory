## Scope and expected use

This policy applies to employees, contractors and third parties using Meridian identities, networks, devices, applications or information. Meridian technology is provided for authorized business activity. Limited personal use is permitted when lawful, reasonable, low-risk and non-disruptive, but users should not expect personal material stored on Meridian systems to remain outside legitimate security, legal or operational review.

Users must not disable security controls, share credentials, evade monitoring, install unauthorized software, operate unapproved network services, conduct harassment, access unlawful material or use Meridian resources for outside commercial activity. Security monitoring is proportionate to threat detection, system reliability, investigation and legal obligations. Monitoring data is access-controlled and is not a general-purpose employee productivity score.

## Managed endpoints

Confidential or Restricted information is accessed from a managed device unless a documented exception provides equivalent controls. Managed devices use supported software, encryption, screen lock, endpoint protection and automated patching. Users restart devices when required for security updates and do not postpone a critical update beyond the published deadline.

Administrative rights are granted only where job duties require them. Software comes from approved catalogs or an IT-reviewed installation. Removable media is blocked or encrypted according to data classification. Users do not connect unknown devices to Meridian equipment or attempt repair that could destroy evidence after a suspected security event.

Lost, stolen or unattended devices and suspected credential exposure are reported immediately through the security channel. The report includes the device, location, time, data likely involved and contact method. Users should not delay reporting while attempting recovery. IT may remotely lock or erase a managed device when risk warrants it.

## Remote access and collaboration

Remote access requires multi-factor authentication and approved connectivity. Restricted administrative access uses a separate elevated identity under IT-002. Users verify unexpected authentication prompts and report repeated prompts they did not initiate. Public or shared computers are not used for Meridian access.

Work files remain in approved collaboration and storage services. Forwarding to personal email, consumer file sharing or an unapproved messaging service is prohibited even when a deadline is at risk. IT Service Management provides approved transfer, guest-access or offline-work options. Confidential conversations require reasonable privacy and approved recording practices.

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exception requests identify the business need, data classification, device or service, duration, owner and proposed controls. IT Service Management records the request, Information Security assesses risk and the business owner accepts residual risk. Approval expires on the recorded date and does not extend to a different device or use case.

Meridian may isolate equipment, suspend access or preserve evidence during an incident. Good-faith reporting of an accidental violation is considered in the response. Deliberate control bypass, credential sharing or concealment may lead to disciplinary or contractual action.
