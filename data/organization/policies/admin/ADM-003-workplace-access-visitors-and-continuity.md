## Workplace access

Access to Meridian workplaces is based on current employment or contractual need, site assignment and area authorization. Badges are individual and must not be lent, copied or used to admit another person. A colleague who forgot a badge reports to Reception or Security for identity verification and approved temporary access.

Lost badges and suspicious access events are reported immediately. Employees display or carry identification as the site requires and challenge or safely report tailgating, propped secure doors and attempts to bypass controls. Safety comes first; employees should not physically confront a person.

Restricted areas such as network rooms, records storage, laboratories or executive incident rooms require explicit authorization. General site access does not imply restricted-area access. Facilities and Security review access periodically and remove it when duties or location change.

## Visitors and third parties

Hosts register visitors before arrival where practicable, identify the business purpose and select the correct access level. Visitors verify identity, wear the issued credential and are escorted according to area risk. Hosts remain accountable for the visitor, including discussion topics, photography, documents and device connections.

Delivery personnel and service vendors use designated routes and work areas. A contract or work order does not by itself authorize system, data or restricted-area access. ADM-001 governs vendor onboarding; IT-002 governs digital identities. Visitor records are retained only for security, safety and legal needs under IT-003.

## Safety and incident reporting

Employees follow evacuation, shelter, medical and site-specific safety instructions. Hazards, threats, injuries, access failures and suspicious packages are reported through the published channel. In an immediate emergency, local emergency services and site alarms take precedence over routine management escalation.

The incident commander may establish temporary access, closure, communication or assembly rules. These rules are limited to the event and do not permanently amend policy. Only authorized communicators issue organization-wide safety instructions. Employees should not share unverified incident details or personal information.

## Business continuity

Each critical business function maintains an owner, deputy, contact tree, minimum service, critical dependencies, recovery steps and records needed during disruption. Plans address loss of site, people, technology, supplier and communications—not only building evacuation. Teams test plans on a risk-based schedule and record gaps, actions and owners.

A site closure does not automatically mean every service continues normally. Leaders declare priorities, paused work, customer commitments and reporting cadence. HR-001 governs temporary work-location arrangements, while IT-001 governs remote access. Staff should not improvise unsafe workarounds or move Restricted information to personal services because the normal site is unavailable.

## Exceptions, privacy and review

Facilities approves routine physical-access exceptions; Security reviews restricted-area or elevated-risk requests. Each exception identifies person, area, purpose, sponsor, start, expiry and escort or monitoring controls. Emergency access is reviewed afterward.

Badge and visitor logs are security records, not general productivity data. Access is limited to authorized Facilities, Security, Legal or investigation personnel. Facilities owns site controls, business leaders own continuity plans, and all employees own prompt reporting and compliance with emergency direction.
