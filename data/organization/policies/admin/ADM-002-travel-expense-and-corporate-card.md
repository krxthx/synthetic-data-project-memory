## Principles and approval

Meridian reimburses necessary, reasonable and properly documented business costs. Travelers are expected to consider safety, accessibility, sustainability, total trip cost and productive use of time. The lowest nominal fare is not always the reasonable option when it introduces material risk, excessive journey time or restrictive change conditions.

Travel requires the approval defined for the employee's business unit before booking. The request states purpose, destination, dates, expected cost and customer or event relationship. Approval of a trip does not approve every later expense. Travel is booked through approved channels unless safety, disruption or availability requires an exception.

## Booking and conduct

Travelers select reasonable transport and accommodation within published regional guidance. Upgrades, companion travel, leisure extensions and personal changes are paid personally unless an approved business or accessibility need applies. When business and personal travel are combined, Meridian pays no more than the documented business-only equivalent and does not assume liability for the personal portion.

Elevated-risk destinations use the security travel review. Travelers maintain current contact details, follow emergency guidance and avoid placing sensitive information at risk in public settings. Lost devices follow IT-001 immediately, even outside normal business hours.

## Expenses and receipts

Claims are submitted within thirty calendar days and identify date, amount, currency, merchant, attendees where relevant, business purpose, project or cost center and required receipt. Missing-receipt declarations are exceptional and must describe attempts to obtain evidence. Repeated missing receipts are reviewed.

Meals, local transport, connectivity, visas and other costs are reimbursed according to regional rules and actual business need. Alcohol, gifts and entertainment require compliance with applicable limits and anti-bribery guidance. Commuting to a normal work location is generally personal; travel to a temporary business location may be eligible under local rules.

An employee cannot approve their own claim. Approvers verify purpose, reasonableness, evidence, allocation and policy exceptions rather than treating approval as a budget check. Finance may correct coding, request evidence, reject a claim or recover an overpayment.

## Corporate cards

Cards are issued to named users for authorized business spend. Card details are protected and are not shared. Cash withdrawal, personal subscriptions and deliberate personal spend are prohibited. An accidental personal charge is reported promptly, repaid through the stated process and not disguised as business expenditure. Repeated or concealed misuse may lead to card suspension and disciplinary review.

## Exceptions and disruption

During cancellations, safety events or severe disruption, travelers prioritize safety and use reasonable judgment. They retain evidence and notify the Travel Program as soon as practicable. Accessibility, health, visa or caregiving constraints are reviewed confidentially with HR where appropriate. Exceptions record the reason, approver and affected transaction; precedent for one trip does not create a permanent entitlement.
