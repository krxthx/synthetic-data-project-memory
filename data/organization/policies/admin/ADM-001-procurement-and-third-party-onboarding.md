## Purpose and scope

This policy governs purchases, subscriptions, professional services and other third-party commitments made on behalf of Meridian Group. It applies whether payment uses a purchase order, invoice, corporate card, expense reimbursement, free trial, credit, barter or another arrangement. Splitting spend, using personal funds or describing a recurring service as a pilot does not remove the requirement.

## Sourcing and authority

A purchase request is submitted before a requester commits Meridian to a supplier. The request defines business need, expected value, total cost, term, funding, alternatives, data access, integrations and accountable contract owner. Competitive sourcing follows the applicable threshold. Procurement may accept documented market research, framework pricing, sole-source capability or emergency circumstances where competition is not practical.

Requesters disclose personal, financial or family relationships with a supplier and withdraw from conflicted decisions. A requester cannot approve their own spend. Only delegated signatories may accept terms, sign documents or click an online acceptance that binds Meridian. Email enthusiasm, budget availability or successful technical testing is not contractual authority.

## Risk review and onboarding

Third parties that access Meridian systems, facilities, Confidential or Restricted data undergo appropriate security, privacy, legal, finance and continuity review before access. Review depth reflects data, connectivity, criticality, geography, subcontractors and replacement difficulty. The vendor must not receive production credentials while review or contract terms remain incomplete.

The contract owner confirms deliverables, service levels, data use, audit rights, incident notification, insurance, intellectual property, termination assistance, data return or deletion and renewal terms. IT-002 governs identities and IT-003 governs data handling. Visitors and on-site suppliers also follow ADM-003.

A software trial may begin before full vendor onboarding only when no Meridian or personal data is entered, no production integration is enabled, no automatic renewal or payment commitment exists and Procurement has not otherwise required review. A useful trial does not create retroactive permission to upload data.

## Performance, renewal and offboarding

The contract owner monitors service, issues, spend, risk and business value. Renewal review starts early enough to avoid automatic renewal pressure. Material scope, data, hosting, ownership or subcontractor changes trigger reassessment rather than relying on the original approval.

Offboarding removes identities, tokens, network access, badges and integrations; retrieves Meridian assets; confirms data return or deletion; transfers necessary records; and resolves outstanding invoices. Procurement records termination evidence and any continuing obligations.

## Exceptions and records

Emergency or sole-source requests document urgency, alternatives, reason competition was impractical, pricing evidence and residual risk. Procurement, Finance and the relevant risk owner approve before commitment whenever practicable. Emergency approval is not permission to skip later contract and offboarding controls.

The procurement record contains the request, bids or justification, conflicts disclosure, reviews, approvals, executed agreement, performance evidence, changes, renewal decision and offboarding result. Records follow IT-003 retention and access rules.
